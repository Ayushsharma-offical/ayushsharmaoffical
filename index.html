sar-drone — search & rescue

A brushless-motor drone built for disaster response — think earthquakes, where responders need to know where people are trapped before they go in. A Raspberry Pi and camera onboard run a trained model that flags possible survivors in the footage and marks their approximate location, for police or fire and rescue teams on the ground to act on.

Raspberry Pi Brushless motors Computer vision Disaster response
running at home
fire-safety-relay — short circuit emergency response

A safety layer for the home automation setup that watches for a short circuit or sudden current spike. When it trips: power to the house lighting circuit cuts immediately, a buzzer sounds, a servo-actuated fire extinguisher release fires near the panel, and an automatic alert goes out toward emergency services. Wired in alongside the ESP32 relay work above and active on the real panel.

ESP32 Current sensing Servo-triggered extinguisher Auto shutoff Emergency alert
running at home
